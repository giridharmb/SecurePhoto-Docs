Private by default

We built Secure Photo so that nobody but you can see your photos, including us.

  • Every photo is encrypted on your device with AES-256 before it is stored or synced.
  • iCloud only ever receives encrypted files. Labels and album names use iCloud's end-to-end encrypted fields.
  • No sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code.
  • We run no servers and never receive your photos or any information about you.
  • Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. The app never sees your face data.
  • You decide what syncs. Turn iCloud sync off at any time in Settings.

Read the privacy policy

Good to know

Photos you add to Secure Photo are automatically synced to your own private iCloud account and use your iCloud storage. You can turn this off in the app's Settings.

Secure Photo is for still photos. Videos aren't supported, and Live Photos are kept as their still image. There's no editing: edit in the Photos app, then move the photo into the vault.

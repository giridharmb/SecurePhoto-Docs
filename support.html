Getting started

How do I move photos into Secure Photo?

Tap + in the Library and choose Choose Photos… or Import Album…. After you pick, Secure Photo asks whether to Import & Delete from Photos or Import & Keep in Photos. If you delete, iOS asks you to confirm, and the originals go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days.

Why does Secure Photo need full access to my photo library?

To delete originals after import and to save photos back, iOS requires full library access. Secure Photo only touches the photos you choose. You can change access in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

How do I move photos back to the Photos app?

Select photos and tap Save to Photos at the bottom, or open one photo and tap the same button in the viewer. For an album, open it and use ⋯ › Save Album to Photos…. Each time you choose Save & Remove from Secure Photo or Save & Keep.

Does Secure Photo support videos or editing?

No. Secure Photo is for still photos, and Live Photos are kept as their still image. To edit, move a photo back to the Photos app, edit it there, then move it in again.

Unlocking

Face ID doesn't appear

Make sure Face ID is set up in iOS Settings and allowed for Secure Photo (Settings › Face ID & Passcode › Other Apps). If Face ID isn't available, your device passcode is used.

Can I stop it locking every time I switch apps?

Yes. In Secure Photo's Settings › Privacy › Require Face ID, choose After 1 Minute or After 5 Minutes.

iCloud sync

Where are my photos synced?

To your own private iCloud account, encrypted before upload. They use your iCloud storage. See the privacy policy for details.

Photos aren't syncing
  • Check you're signed in to iCloud on the device.
  • In Secure Photo's Settings, make sure iCloud Sync is on.
  • Syncing uses Wi-Fi only by default. To sync on mobile data, turn on Use Cellular Data.
  • Sync runs while the app is open. Pull down on the Library, or tap Sync Now.
  • Check that your iCloud storage isn't full.
Setting up a second iPhone or iPad

Install Secure Photo, sign in to the same Apple ID, open the app and let it sync before you import anything new on that device. It picks up your vault key and photos from iCloud automatically.

"This device's vault key differs from the one in iCloud"

This happens if you imported photos on a new device before its first sync. Sync is paused so nothing is lost. Export a backup on that device (Settings › Backup), delete and reinstall Secure Photo, let it sync, then restore the backup.

Organising

How do favorites, stars and 🔥 work?

In the photo viewer, tap a star to rate (tap it again to clear), tap 🔥 to mark a photo hot, and tap the heart for favorites. In the grid, long-press any photo for the same options, or select several and use the tag button.

Why did my hashtag change?

Hashtags use only lowercase a–z and 0–9. Capitals are lowered, accents removed, and other characters dropped, so #Beach-Trip_2024! becomes #beachtrip2024. The editor shows exactly what will be saved before you add it.

How do I find photos quickly?

Use the filter buttons above the grid: tap ❤️, 🔥, ⭐️, a color or a hashtag, and combine as many as you like. Type in the search bar for suggestions. Tap Save to keep a combination; saved filters appear in the Browse tab.

How do I select lots of photos at once?

Tap Select, then drag your finger sideways across thumbnails. Pinch the grid to make thumbnails bigger or smaller.

Deleting & backups

I deleted a photo by mistake

Go to Browse › Recently Deleted, select it and tap Recover. Photos stay there for 30 days.

How do I back up without iCloud?
  1. Open Settings › Backup › Export Encrypted Backup…
  2. Choose a password of at least 8 characters.
  3. Tap Save Backup to Files… and save the .spbackup file to Files, a drive or your computer.

To restore, use Restore from Backup… and enter the same password. Photos already in your vault are skipped.

I forgot my backup password

Unfortunately, it can't be recovered. The password is never stored anywhere, which is what keeps the backup safe. Make a new backup with a password you'll remember.
